Privacy

Global Privacy Control sends a standard opt-out signal; websites decide whether to honour it. Clearing removes cookies, site storage and cached files for every website and signs you out. Pages that are loading or playing stop and may need a reload. History and bookmarks are kept.

Diagnostics

Off by default. Logs may contain visited addresses. Clearing logs turns diagnostics off and removes only OrbisFox diagnostic files; your profile, history and bookmarks are kept.
